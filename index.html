FAQ

Before you start planning.

01What does Sceneory actually do?

Sceneory turns scripts into structured visual production plans containing scene directions, research and search queries, visual types, and editing guidance.

02Does Sceneory download footage automatically?

No. Sceneory helps you plan and locate visuals. You choose and download the media yourself.

03Does Sceneory include an AI subscription?

No. Supported cloud AI providers use your own API configuration. Any provider costs are separate from Sceneory.

04Which AI providers are supported?

OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, and Ollama.

05Can I use Sceneory with local AI?

Yes. Sceneory supports local Ollama models.

06Where are my projects stored?

Projects are stored locally on your computer.

07Does Sceneory send analytics?

Sceneory currently does not include analytics or telemetry.

08Does Sceneory work on macOS?

Not currently. Sceneory is currently available for Windows.
